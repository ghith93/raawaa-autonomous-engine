# Mobile-only arbitrage research

Research date: 2026-11-02.

The system must be operated from iPhone and Lenovo tablet only. Compute, tests, market scans and durable state must run in cloud services; neither device may be a required worker.

The current free architecture is periodic and bounded, not a guaranteed 24/7 low-latency trading service. Do not claim profitability from gross spreads, simulated reserves or backtests alone.

See the current ecosystem review and issue #175 for the market-family roadmap.
